Pourlore – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Pourlore (Android package com.shazaib.pourlore), published by Shazaib Sarwar ("we", "us"), handles information. Pourlore is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information. We run no servers and no analytics for this game.

Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset progress deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pourlore shows ads from Google AdMob, including banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): Pourlore asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can decline personalised ads and still play the full game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).
  • Offline play: the game works fully without an internet connection, and no ads load while you are offline.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the ad data processed by Google AdMob described above, no data leaves your device.

Children

Pourlore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through ads, contact us and we will help you ask Google to remove it.

Security and retention

Game progress stays on your device under Android's app sandbox. Google keeps ad data according to its own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
